# Warranty

## Warranty period
All electronics carry a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects, starting on the delivery date.

## What is not covered
Accidental damage, liquid damage, wear and tear and unauthorised repairs are not covered by the warranty.

## Making a warranty claim
Contact support with your order number and a short description or photo of the defect. If the claim is approved we send a replacement or repair the item free of charge.

## Replacement items
A replacement item is covered for the remainder of the original warranty period.
