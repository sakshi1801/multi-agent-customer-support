# Payments

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, UPI and PayPal. We do not accept cash on delivery, cheques or American Express.

## When you are charged
Your card is charged when the order ships, not when you place it. UPI and PayPal payments are collected at checkout and refunded automatically if the order is cancelled before shipping.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, no order is created. Try again or use another method. If money left your account but no order appears, it is returned within 5-7 business days.

## Invoices and tax
A GST invoice is emailed when the order ships. Prices at checkout include all taxes.

## Promo codes
Only one promo code can be used per order, and codes cannot be applied after the order is placed.

## Refunds to your account
Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5-7 business days to show up after we receive a returned item.
