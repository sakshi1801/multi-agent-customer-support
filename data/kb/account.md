# Account

## Resetting your password
Use "Forgot password" on the login page. A reset link is emailed to you and stays valid for 30 minutes.

## Changing your email address
Your email address can be changed from the account settings page. Orders already placed stay linked to the old address until support moves them.

## Deleting your account
You can ask support to delete your account. Order records are kept for 7 years for tax reasons, but your personal profile is removed.

## Privacy
We never share your details with other customers, and support staff see only the information needed to help you. Customers can only see their own orders.
