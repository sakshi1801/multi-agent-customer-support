# Shipping

## Standard shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days after the order has shipped. It is the default option at checkout.

## Express shipping
Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs extra. The exact fee is shown at checkout before you pay.

## Free shipping
Orders over $50 ship free with standard shipping. Express shipping is never free.

## Order processing time
Orders are packed and handed to the courier within 1 business day. Orders placed on Sundays and public holidays are processed on the next business day.

## Tracking your shipment
Once an order ships you receive an email with a tracking link. You can also ask the support assistant for the status of any of your orders.

## International shipping
We currently deliver only within India. International shipping is not available yet.

## Delivery attempts
The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. After the third failed attempt the parcel is returned to us and you are refunded, minus any express shipping fee.

## Delayed deliveries
If your order has not arrived 3 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support and we will trace the parcel with the courier.

## Changing the delivery address
The address can be changed only while the order status is "processing". Once an order has shipped the address cannot be changed.
