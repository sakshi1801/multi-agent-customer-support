# Contact

## Support hours
Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm IST. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Response time for escalations
Escalated issues get a response within one business day. Urgent issues such as fraud are handled first.

## Talking to a human
Ask the assistant for a human agent at any time. It will create a support ticket with your conversation attached, so you do not need to repeat yourself.
