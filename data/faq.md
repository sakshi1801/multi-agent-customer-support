# Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs extra. Orders over $50 ship free.

# Returns
You can return any unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Start a return from the Orders page and print the prepaid label. Refunds appear within 5-7 business days after we receive the item.

# Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled while their status is "processing". Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but you can return it after delivery.

# Payments
We accept Visa, Mastercard, UPI and PayPal. Your card is charged when the order ships, not when you place it.

# Warranty
All electronics carry a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Accidental damage is not covered.

# Contact
Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm IST. Escalated issues get a response within one business day.
